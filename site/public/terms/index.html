Block Brief

Terms of service

Effective 2026-11-01. Block Brief is made by Brick and Mortar AI LLC, Saint Paul, Minnesota.

What it is

Block Brief is a weekly email for one shop. It reads the sales your register already records and the public records the City of Minneapolis and Hennepin County already publish about the ground within 500 feet of your door, and writes one short briefing from them every Monday.

Your register

You connect a Square or Clover account. Block Brief asks for read-only access to your merchant profile, payments and orders. It never writes to your register, never moves money, and never sees card numbers. You can revoke access at any time from your Square dashboard (Apps) or your Clover dashboard (More Tools), and the next briefing will not be sent.

What we keep

Your email, your shop's name and address, the parcel your door sits on, the daily totals and counts we read each week, the briefings we send you, and your access tokens, which are encrypted at rest. Nothing is sold and nothing is shared with anyone else. Privacy says the same in full.

Paying and stopping

$29 a month, billed through the Clover App Market if you installed from there, otherwise through Stripe on this site. No trial, no tiers, no minimum term. Cancel from your Clover dashboard or from the link on your receipt; the charge stops at the end of the paid month.

What the briefing is

Every figure in a briefing is read from your register or from a public record, and the record is named. The prose that joins them is written by a language model from those figures only. It is information about your block, not legal, tax or financial advice; a county value or an appeal deadline is the county's, and the county's own notice governs.
